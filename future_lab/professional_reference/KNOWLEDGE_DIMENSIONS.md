# FATHER — Universal Professional Knowledge Dimensions

Every profession is described through the same dimensions where applicable.

## K1 Fundamental knowledge
- mathematics
- logic
- statistics
- physics/chemistry/biology where relevant
- computer science where relevant
- language and communication foundations

## K2 Domain theory
The body of knowledge specific to the profession.

## K3 Methods
Recognized ways of solving professional problems.

## K4 Algorithms and decision logic
Formal and heuristic decision procedures.

## K5 Standards and regulation
Laws, standards, professional frameworks, normative requirements.

## K6 Tools
Software, hardware, instruments, platforms and services.

## K7 Procedures
Repeatable professional workflows.

## K8 Quality
How output quality is measured and accepted.

## K9 Safety and security
Professional, operational, information, physical and legal safety.

## K10 Economics and constraints
Cost, resources, time, efficiency and trade-offs.

## K11 Operations
Deployment, maintenance, monitoring, incident response and lifecycle.

## K12 Communication
Documentation, reporting, stakeholder interaction, handoffs.

## K13 Practice and experience
Cases, failures, anti-patterns, diagnostics, recovery.

## K14 Ethics and responsibility
Professional boundaries, acceptable use, escalation and accountability.

## K15 Qualification
Exercises, labs, exams, supervised practice and production evidence.
