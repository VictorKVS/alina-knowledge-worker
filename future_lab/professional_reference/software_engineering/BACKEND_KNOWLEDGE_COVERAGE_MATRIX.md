## Stop condition

AK-01 stops acquiring additional professional knowledge when:
1. all mandatory areas reach required depth;
2. all task/decision mappings are covered;
3. all critical scenarios pass;
4. no unresolved critical knowledge gaps remain;
5. qualification evidence meets the role threshold.

More documents are not automatically better.
