# FATHER — Knowledge Depth Model

K0 AWARENESS
Knows that the concept exists.

K1 UNDERSTANDING
Can explain mechanism and terminology.

K2 APPLICATION
Can apply in a standard case.

K3 ANALYSIS
Can compare alternatives and recognize limitations.

K4 DESIGN
Can design a solution under constraints.

K5 EXPERT
Can diagnose, optimize, handle novel cases and teach/review others.

## Sufficiency
A knowledge area is sufficient only when:
- required depth is reached;
- required tasks are covered;
- decision scenarios pass;
- practical evidence passes;
- critical safety/security gates pass.
