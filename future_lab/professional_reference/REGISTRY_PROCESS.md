# FATHER — Professional Reference Ingestion Process

## Stage 1 — Profession discovery
Identify role, aliases, adjacent professions and specializations.

## Stage 2 — Reference acquisition
Collect authoritative professional frameworks, bodies of knowledge, standards, regulations, curricula and official documentation.

## Stage 3 — Task extraction
Extract typical professional tasks.

## Stage 4 — Decision extraction
For every task identify decisions the professional must make independently.

## Stage 5 — Competency derivation
Map tasks and decisions to competencies.

## Stage 6 — Knowledge derivation
For every competency derive required knowledge and prerequisite graph.

## Stage 7 — Depth sizing
Assign K0-K5 depth according to responsibility and product quality requirements.

## Stage 8 — Skill mapping
Define practical actions and tool skills.

## Stage 9 — Quality/safety gates
Define acceptance, safety, security and escalation requirements.

## Stage 10 — Qualification design
Define labs, cases, adversarial cases, exams and production evidence.

## Stage 11 — Versioning
Publish Profession Reference vN.

## Continuous update
Production failures, technology changes and standards changes produce Profession Delta proposals.
