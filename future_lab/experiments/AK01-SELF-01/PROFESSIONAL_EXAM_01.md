# AK01-SELF-01 — Professional Diagnostic Exam 01

## Goal

Move from testing the factory machinery to testing AK-01 as a professional digital specialist.

## Principle

The exam is specified before any corrective training iteration.

A diagnostic pass is not production certification.

## Exam ladder

- E1 KNOWLEDGE
- E2 DECISION
- E3 PROCEDURE
- E4 FAILURE
- E5 ADVERSARIAL
- E6 CAPSTONE

## First diagnostic scope

AK-01 must demonstrate that it can:

1. identify a professional knowledge need from a task;
2. separate discovery from qualification;
3. choose an evidence route;
4. preserve counter-evidence and limitations;
5. assemble a traceable knowledge unit;
6. avoid self-qualification;
7. stop when evidence is insufficient;
8. produce a training delta from failed criteria.

## Qualification boundary

PASS → candidate may move to SHADOW PRODUCTION.

PASS does not mean CERTIFIED FOR PRODUCTION.

FAIL_DIAGNOSTIC → failed criteria become explicit training gaps.
