# Website Qualification

A website is evaluated by ownership, purpose, evidence and claim scope — not by visual quality or search rank.

## Identity / ownership
- accountable organization
- named author/editor where relevant
- institutional contact
- stable domain
- publication/update date
- version/history if material changes occur

## Authority by claim type
Examples:
- vendor documentation: authoritative about that vendor's API/product behavior
- regulator website: authoritative about regulator publications/actions
- standards body: authoritative about its own standards
- university page: potentially authoritative only within the represented academic context
- company blog: practitioner evidence, not automatically scientific evidence

## Evidence transparency
Check:
- cited primary sources
- datasets/code
- methodology
- conflicts/commercial interest
- correction policy
- update history

## Freshness
For dynamic technical topics, stale pages are downgraded even if historically accurate.

## Verdict
Official origin does not make every statement universally authoritative.
Authority is always scoped to the issuer's competence and the claim.
