# AK-01 — Source Classes

## A0 Normative / Official Primary
Examples:
- legislation
- official standards
- RFC/specification
- vendor official technical documentation for its own product
- official dataset / registry

Use:
authoritative for facts within issuing body's scope.

## A1 Scientific Primary
Original peer-reviewed research with inspectable methods/results, or a clearly identified version of record.

Use:
primary evidence for scientific/technical claims.

## A2 Scientific Synthesis
Systematic review, meta-analysis, consensus report, evidence-based guideline, authoritative body of knowledge.

Use:
high-value synthesis; may outrank single studies for broad conclusions.

## A3 Scholarly Book / Monograph
Academic/professional monograph with identifiable author/editor, reputable scholarly/technical publisher, references and traceable edition.

Use:
foundations, mature theory, methods, synthesis.

## A4 Authoritative Professional Reference
Professional association, standards body, recognized institutional guidance, canonical technical documentation.

Use:
professional methods and accepted practice.

## B1 High-quality Practitioner Evidence
Engineering case study, benchmark, incident report, reputable technical publication with transparent methods.

Use:
implementation and operational evidence; not sole basis for foundational scientific claims.

## B2 Preprint / Working Paper
Scientific-looking work not yet established as peer-reviewed version of record.

Use:
current discovery and provisional evidence, always labelled.

## C Discovery
Blogs, forums, Q&A, social media, SEO pages, unverified tutorials.

Use:
lead generation only unless independently corroborated.

## Rule
Class is not a permanent prestige badge. It is assigned to a specific source version and may change after correction, retraction or review.
