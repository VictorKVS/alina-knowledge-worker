# Book / Monograph Qualification

There is no universal mechanism by which ISBN alone certifies scientific quality.
ISBN identifies an edition/publication; it does not prove rigor.

## Identity
- author/editor
- qualifications and relevant expertise
- title
- edition
- year
- ISBN / DOI if present
- publisher
- translation status
- original language/edition

## Publisher / editorial quality
Prefer:
- academic/university presses
- recognized professional societies
- established technical/scientific publishers
- transparent editorial/review process where available

Publisher reputation alone is not sufficient.

## Content quality
Check:
- explicit scope
- references/bibliography
- references to primary literature
- separation of evidence vs author opinion
- methodological clarity
- exercises/examples where relevant
- errata
- edition currency
- consistency with current standards/research

## Use policy
Books are strongest for:
- foundations
- stable theory
- mature methods
- structured learning

Books may be weak for:
- fast-moving software/tool versions
- current vulnerabilities
- current regulations
- frontier research

For fast-moving claims, pair book knowledge with current primary/official sources.
