# AK-01 — Source Qualification: Gates and Scoring

## Principle
Hard gates first. Scoring only after hard disqualifiers are cleared.

## Hard gates
G1 identity verified
G2 version known
G3 integrity status checked
G4 provenance retrievable
G5 no release-blocking deception/retraction condition

Failure:
QUARANTINE or REJECT.

## Evaluation dimensions
Each dimension is recorded separately; AK-01 must not hide them behind one unexplained score.

- authority
- methodological_rigor
- transparency
- reproducibility
- relevance
- applicability
- recency
- independence
- conflict_of_interest
- external_support
- editorial_quality
- integrity_status

## Optional derived ranking
A policy may derive a ranking score only for candidate ordering.
The raw dimensions remain visible and auditable.

## Critical rule
A highly cited weak study does not automatically beat a rigorous lower-cited study.
A new source does not automatically beat a mature source.
A prestigious venue does not automatically validate an unsuitable method.
