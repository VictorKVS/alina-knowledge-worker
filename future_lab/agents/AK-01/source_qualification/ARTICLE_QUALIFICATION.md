# Scientific Article Qualification

## Gate 1 — Identity
Check:
- title
- authors
- affiliations
- venue
- publication date
- DOI or equivalent persistent identifier when expected
- publication type
- version: preprint / accepted manuscript / version of record

DOI is an identifier, not a quality guarantee.

## Gate 2 — Venue integrity
Check:
- transparent editorial board
- transparent peer-review policy
- publication ethics policies
- correction/retraction policy
- recognized indexing/selection where domain-relevant
- no deceptive/predatory signals

## Gate 3 — Research integrity status
Check:
- retraction
- correction
- expression of concern
- replacement/update
- duplicate/preprint relation

## Gate 4 — Methodological fitness
Assess relative to study type:
- clear research question
- appropriate design
- sample/data adequacy
- controls/comparators where required
- statistical/analytical appropriateness
- reproducibility details
- uncertainty reporting
- limitations
- data/code availability where relevant

## Gate 5 — Claim fit
A paper may support only claims actually tested by its design.
Do not promote author speculation/discussion into established fact.

## Gate 6 — External support
Check:
- replication or independent support
- systematic reviews/meta-analyses
- later contradictory evidence
- post-publication corrections/comments

## Output
Store:
- article class
- peer_review_status
- version_status
- integrity_status
- methodology_assessment
- applicability
- limitations
- supported_claims
- unsupported_claims
